Memorable Events

This page is an effort to document the author's memorable events for posterity. The author has over 200,000+ photos and hundreds of videos that have been taken over the years. One day, AI will make these easily searchable and accessible. Until then, this page will at least show the highlights and directs the authorized visitor to personal folders on the author's cloud storage for the rest of the photos or videos of the events.

Photos or videos without context are less interesting. This page recreates some context for the events for which the photos and videos were taken. In addition to providing a list of events, the table below also provides additional details on each event that can be accessed by clicking on the "date" of the event.

Additional links to the external cloud storage folders for the photos and videos are provided in the sidebar .

If you don't have shared access priviledge to the cloud storage folders and you would like to access the folder(s), contact the author for the access credentials.
