Robert Tong (Site Author)

Son. Father. Engineer

Robert Shun-Keung Tong was born in Hong Kong in 1959. He is second of four children of William Tong and Chei-Wen Tong. Both his parents migrated from Shanghai, China in the 1950s.

Robert left Hong Kong for Toronto, Canada in 1976 to pursue his studies. After graduating with a Bachelor of Engineering degree from McMaster University in 1981, he went to work for COM DEV International Ltd, which was a communications satellite equipment manufacturer in Cambridge, Ontario, Canada. In 2000, he switched from the satellite industry to work as President and CEO of a semiconductor startup in Waterloo, Ontario, Canada. He remained in the semiconductor industry till his retirement in 2023. He last job was with ON Semiconductor in Phoenix, Arizona as senior vice president of its Advanced Solutions Group.

Married to Stella Tong since 1980, they have four children together. Robert and Stella migrated to the US in 2019 for his job. Today, they enjoy their retirement by splitting time between Canada and the US.
